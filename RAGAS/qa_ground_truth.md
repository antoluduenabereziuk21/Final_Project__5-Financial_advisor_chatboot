# Question–answer ground truth

**Q: How much did AAPL spend on stock buybacks in fiscal 2022?**
A: $90.2 billion repurchased in FY2022 (Apple Inc. 10-K, Item 7 MD&A).

**Q: What was AAPL's quarterly dividend per share starting in May 2022?**
A: Raised from $0.22 to $0.23 per share, effective May 2022.

**Q: What was AMAT's total backlog as of October 30, 2022?**
A: $19,011 million total backlog; approximately 32% not expected to be filled within the next 12 months; new export rules issued December 2022 were expected to reduce backlog by approximately $989 million.

**Q: What was Applied Materials' backlog at the end of fiscal 2022?**
A: $19,011 million (same fact as above).

**Q: What percentage of Applied Optoelectronics' revenue in fiscal 2022 was denominated in RMB?**
A: 4.7% of FY2022 revenue was denominated in RMB; 0% was denominated in NT dollars (Item 7A).

**Q: What percentage of Applied Optoelectronics' revenue was in RMB in 2022?**
A: 4.7% (same fact as above).

**Q: What did American Airlines say about depreciating its aircraft?**
A: Residual values for aircraft, engines, and related rotable parts are generally 5%–10% of original cost — consistent across the FY2020, FY2021, and FY2022 10-Ks.

**Q: Tell me about Antares Pharma's LEO Pharma agreement.**
A: In November 2013, Antares granted LEO Pharma the exclusive US right to promote OTREXUP™ to dermatologists. Antares received a non-refundable $5.0 million upfront payment plus a $5.0 million milestone payment, with a further $10.0 million milestone contingent on reaching a defined net-sales level. The payments were treated as a single unit of accounting, recognized as revenue over the 35-month estimated life of the agreement: $3,429,000 recognized in FY2014, $571,000 in FY2013 (comparative), with $6,000,000 in deferred revenue remaining at December 31, 2014.

**Q: What is the origin of Ascendis Pharma's goodwill, and has any impairment been recognized?**
A: The goodwill originates from the 2007 acquisition of Complex Biosystems GmbH (now Ascendis Pharma GmbH). No impairments have been recognized in any period presented. (Source chunk is from the FY2021 filing.)

**Q: What milestone payment did argenx receive when LEO Pharma exercised its option in September 2022?**
A: €5.0 million.

**Q: What was Apple's revenue in fiscal 2022?**

**Q: How did Applied Materials Incorporated perform?**
A: Net sales grew from $17,202M (FY2020) to $23,063M (FY2021) to $25,785M (FY2022); operating margin improved from 25.4% to 30.2% over the same period; net income grew from $3,619M to $6,525M.
